ConcertCam Dual Camera App

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

The short version: ConcertCam does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything you record stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. ConcertCam does not gather any personal data, and it does not transmit anything off your device. There is no user account, and the app does not connect to any server we operate or to third-party analytics or advertising services.

Device permissions and how they're used

ConcertCam asks for the following permissions solely to provide its features. The data these permissions access is processed on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Camera & Microphone — used to record video and audio. Recording happens entirely on your device.
  • Photo Library — used to save your finished recordings to your own Photos library.
  • Location (optional) — if you allow it, ConcertCam tags your recordings with the location where they were filmed. That location is stored in the recording's metadata within your own Photos library. It is not collected by us. You can turn this off in the system Settings app at any time.

Where your recordings go

Your recordings are saved to your device's Photos library. From there, you are in control of them — including any sharing, backup (such as iCloud Photos), or deletion, all of which are governed by your own device and iCloud settings and Apple's policies, not by ConcertCam.

Data sharing

Because ConcertCam does not collect any data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or disclose to third parties.

Children's privacy

ConcertCam does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version at this URL and revise the effective date above.

Contact
